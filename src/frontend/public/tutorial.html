A step by step walkthrough of the Hyvmind app.
From Buzz to Trust.
STEP 1

Hyvmind is a note-sharing app. Earn Buzz and use it to publish graphs.

STEP 2

Create a fresh top-level folder or import existing notes from your device.

STEP 3

Hyvmind follows a four-folder hierarchy. The top-level folder is called a Curation. Think of it as a broad category or area of law.

STEP 4

The second level is called a Swarm. Use it for statutes, caselaws, reports or general themes.

STEP 5

The third level is called a Location. Use it for important paragraphs, sections, orders, chapters etc.

STEP 6

The fourth level is both important and subjective. It's called a Law Token. Use it to store the most important pieces of legal language in that Location. (e.g. 'equality before the law', 'securitisation' etc.)

STEP 7

Write your interpretations inside the fourth folder.

STEP 8

If you're an Obsidian user, download the Hyvmind plugin for direct uploads.

STEP 9

The Notes tab has a built-in markdown editor. Use it to write fresh notes, edit existing ones or update metadata.

STEP 10

You can also use the editor to add internal cross-references (by typing '{@').

STEP 11

Attach sources and attributes to any folder by creating _sources.md and _attributes.md files. Sources and attributes of a parent folder are automatically applied to subfolders.

STEP 12

When you're done making Notes, right-click the Curation folder and convert it into a Graph.

STEP 13

Go to the Graphs tab to preview each node and connection. (Note: Graphs don't have to be complete, just accurate.)

STEP 14

When you're happy with a graph, publish it so others can save and extend it.

STEP 15

Go to the Public page to see what others have published. When someone saves your work, you'll earn Trust.
