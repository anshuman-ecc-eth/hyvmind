# Tutorial Steps

**Subtitle:** A step by step walkthrough of the Hyvmind app. From Buzz to Trust.

---

**Step 1** — Hyvmind is a note-sharing app. Earn Buzz and use it to publish graphs.

**Step 2** — Create a fresh top-level folder or import existing notes from your device.

**Step 3** — Hyvmind follows a four-folder hierarchy. The top-level folder is called a Curation. Think of it as a broad category or area of law.

**Step 4** — The second level is called a Swarm. Use it for statutes, caselaws, reports or general themes.

**Step 5** — The third level is called a Location. Use it for important paragraphs, sections, orders, chapters etc.

**Step 6** — The fourth level is both important and subjective. It's called a Law Token. Use it to store the most important pieces of legal language in that Location. (e.g. 'equality before the law', 'securitisation' etc.)

**Step 7** — Write your interpretations inside the fourth folder.

**Step 8** — If you're an Obsidian user, download the Hyvmind plugin for direct uploads.

**Step 9** — The Notes tab has a built-in markdown editor. Use it to write fresh notes, edit existing ones or update metadata.

**Step 10** — You can also use the editor to add internal cross-references (by typing '{@').

**Step 11** — Attach sources and attributes to any folder by creating _sources.md and _attributes.md files. Sources and attributes of a parent folder are automatically applied to subfolders.

**Step 12** — When you're done making Notes, right-click the Curation folder and convert it into a Graph.

**Step 13** — Go to the Graphs tab to preview each node and connection. (Note: Graphs don't have to be complete, just accurate.)

**Step 14** — When you're happy with a graph, publish it so others can save and extend it.

**Step 15** — Go to the Public page to see what others have published. When someone saves your work, you'll earn Trust.
